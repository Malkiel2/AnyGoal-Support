The guides below describe AnyGoal 2.0. Your app version is shown in Settings.

↗

Keep your story

Open the backup controls in Settings to export your habits, check-ins, notes, photos and Focus sessions. Save the backup file somewhere you control before moving devices or reinstalling.

∞

Restore your Pro access

Use Restore purchases on the Pro screen while signed in to the store account that made the purchase. Backups contain your habit data; purchase access is restored through your store.

◷

A moment for focus

Start Focus from Today, choose a habit or simply focus, and pause or resume whenever you need. Completed sessions are saved. You decide when to check in to your habit.

▦

Your routine, close by

Open Home Screen widgets in Settings to choose and customize a widget. Add it using your device’s widget gallery. Lock Screen widgets are available on supported iPhones.

Restore a backup

Choose your saved backup using the restore controls in Settings. Review the confirmation before replacing the data on that device. Restored Focus timers stay paused until you resume them.

Keep a streak going

Earn rescues and use them for a missed day when available. Your calendar keeps completed, rescued and skipped days distinct.

Reminders

Enable notifications for AnyGoal in your device settings, then choose your reminder times in the app. Focus completion alerts are optional.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel recurring purchases through the store that bills you. Removing the app does not cancel a subscription.

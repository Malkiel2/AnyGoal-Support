Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains how AnyGoal, provided by the developer Malkiel, handles information when you use the app and this support website. Contact us at malkiel1234@gmail.com.

Your habits and backups

Habit names, check-ins, notes, photos, streak history and Focus sessions are stored in the app’s local storage on your device. AnyGoal does not operate a cloud habit-history account or automatically upload this content to our servers.

When you export or share a backup or photo, you choose the destination. That destination handles the file under its own policies. Exported backup files are not encrypted by AnyGoal. Restoring a backup replaces local data after confirmation.

Purchases

Apple or Google processes payments. RevenueCat validates purchase records and manages Pro access using purchase and subscription information and an app-user identifier. AnyGoal does not receive your payment-card details. Store accounts manage billing, cancellations and refunds.

Usage and reliability

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app interactions and improve the experience. Firebase Crashlytics reports crashes and errors, including stack traces, app and device information and installation identifiers. Firebase Remote Config uses installation information to supply app settings. Firebase Cloud Messaging supports notification handling and may process app version, installation identifiers and notification interaction metadata. Habit reminders and Focus completion alerts are scheduled locally on your device. These services process technical and usage information; our habit interaction events omit user-entered habit names, notes and photos.

Advertising

The free app can display ads through Google AdMob. When ads are requested, the advertising SDK can process IP-based approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Applicable consent choices and device advertising settings affect this processing. Pro removes ads from the app.

Where required, the app shows Google’s consent form before requesting ads. If privacy options are required for your region, the Privacy option appears in Settings so you can revisit your choices.

Permissions

Photo and camera access supports the optional photo timeline. Notifications support reminders and optional Focus completion alerts. You can manage these permissions in your device’s settings. The app does not request access to your contacts or precise GPS location.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. This website has no analytics or advertising scripts. Firebase Hosting processes technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site.

Storage, security and choices

App data remains on your device until you manage it, replace it with a backup or remove the app and its storage. A backup you exported remains at the destination you selected. Your device’s system backup settings may also include app data; those backups are managed by Apple or Google. Subscription and service records are retained according to the relevant provider’s policies and applicable requirements. Firebase documents that removal of Crashlytics reports and associated identifiers begins after 90 days.

SDK network communications use HTTPS/TLS. Service providers may process information in countries outside your own. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to processing or withdraw consent. Contact malkiel1234@gmail.com with a request; we will assess it and identify the relevant records or provider. Your store controls payment and subscription records.

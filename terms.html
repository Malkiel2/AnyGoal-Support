Subscriptions
& store terms

Last updated: 16 October 2026

AnyGoal offers an optional Pro subscription. The app shows the available plans, localized prices and billing period before you purchase. Pro adds unlimited habits, photo timelines and an ad-free experience.

Billing and renewal

Apple or Google bills purchases through your store account. Recurring plans renew automatically unless canceled through that store. The plan and terms shown in the store’s purchase confirmation apply.

Manage or cancel

Use Manage subscription in the Pro area, or open your store’s subscription settings. Uninstalling AnyGoal does not cancel a subscription.

  • Manage Apple subscriptions
  • Manage Google Play subscriptions

Restore purchases

Use Restore purchases in AnyGoal while signed in to the store account that made the purchase. Restoring a habit backup and restoring store purchases are separate actions.

Store agreements

App downloads and purchases are subject to the relevant store’s agreements and policies.

  • Apple Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement
  • Google Play Terms of Service
